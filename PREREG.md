# CBIO046T-S "partial: five-fold expansion claim not testable" - preregistration (before any model scored)
Parent CBIO046T "Expanding the Druggable Human Proteome Five-Fold". A five-fold expansion needs structure-based pocket prediction (AlphaFold-scale), which is blocked on this box. This repo only benchmarks a sequence/annotation-based druggability classifier on real public labels. No expansion claim is made or tested.
Data: UniProt reviewed human proteome (20,431 entries). Label: gene has at least one compound binding edge in Hetionet v1.0 (CbG, DrugBank-derived 2016), joined on gene symbol. This is an old, DrugBank-biased label ("has a known drug binder"), not a curated druggability class; Pharos TDL could not be used (API page cap).
Features: log length, amino-acid composition (20), top-300 UniProt keywords (binary), top-60 subcellular location terms. Models: LR (C 0.1) and HistGradientBoosting; baselines: family prior (random split), global rate (cold-family), sequence-only LR.
Splits: random 5-fold; cold-family 5-fold (GroupKFold on UniProt protein-family string; proteins without a family string are singleton groups, fraction reported).
Gates: D3 cold-family AUROC (best model) >= 0.75. D4 same after removing family-revealing keywords (Kinase, Receptor, G-protein coupled receptor, Ion channel, Transferase, Hydrolase, Protease, Oxidoreductase, Transducer, Voltage-gated channel, Ligand-gated ion channel) >= 0.70.
Directions: D1 data and label stats; D2 random split; D3 cold-family; D4 keyword-ablated cold-family; D5 sequence-only cold-family; D6 feature-group comparison (keywords only, location only, sequence only) cold-family; D7 label-shuffle control; D8 per broad class AUROC (kinase, receptor, ion channel, enzyme, other) from cold-family OOF; D9 calibration (Brier, expected calibration error) cold-family; D10 ranked list of currently unlabeled proteins with high predicted probability (counts at p>0.5/0.8, top 25 names) as hypotheses only, NOT validated druggable targets.
